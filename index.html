Why we started

Most online tools ask you to upload your files to a server before they can help you. For something as personal as a document, that never felt right to us.

So we decided to build tools that work on your own device, where your files stay with you. No account, no tracking and no ads.

We are students, and we want software that is simple to use, easy to understand and easy to trust. That is why everything we build is open source, so anyone can see how it works.

What we believe
