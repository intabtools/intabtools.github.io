Open source tools that run entirely on your device.

InTab Tools is a community-driven collection of simple, useful tools for web, desktop and mobile. Processing happens locally, so your files are never uploaded to the cloud or handled by a server.

A privacy-first PDF reader and toolkit. Open, read and edit documents, all processed locally on your device.

No upload step

Tools do their work on your computer or phone. Your files are never sent to a server for processing.

A locked-down desktop app

The InTab PDF desktop app blocks requests to outside websites and won't open other pages or windows.
